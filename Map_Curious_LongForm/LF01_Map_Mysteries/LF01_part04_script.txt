[curious] In eastern Pennsylvania, there's a town where the ground is on fire. [short pause] Centralia sits on top of a coal seam. In 1962, a fire started in the area, possibly while a dump was being burned, and it crept into the old coal mines beneath the town. [serious] Underground, the fire found an endless supply of fuel. Smoke and dangerous gases began rising through cracks in yards and roads. In 1981, a boy fell into a steaming sinkhole that opened beneath him. He survived, but the danger was now impossible to ignore. [confident] In the 1980s, the federal government funded a buyout, and nearly all of the roughly one thousand residents moved away. Most of the houses were torn down. Even the town's ZIP code was later taken away. [curious] Today, Centralia is mostly empty streets and woods. And the fire is still burning, slowly moving through the coal. Some estimates say it could keep going for another two hundred years. From a fire under the ground, to a man who fell out of the sky.
