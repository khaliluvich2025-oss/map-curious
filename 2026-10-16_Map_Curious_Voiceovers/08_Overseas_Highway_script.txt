[short pause] It's the Overseas Highway, linking mainland Florida to Key West through a chain of tiny islands called the Florida Keys. It crosses about forty bridges, including the famous Seven Mile Bridge. [confident] The route began as a railroad. Henry Flagler's Overseas Railroad reached Key West in 1912. But in 1935, a powerful Labor Day hurricane wrecked long sections of it. Instead of rebuilding the trains, Florida turned the route into a road, which opened in 1938. [curious] At the end of the road, Key West sits about ninety miles from Cuba, closer to Havana than to Miami.
