[curious] Seattle can see one of America's most dangerous volcanoes from downtown. [short pause] Mount Rainier rises about fourteen thousand four hundred feet, roughly sixty miles southeast of the city. It holds more glacier ice than any other peak in the lower forty-eight states. [serious] That ice is the danger. If the volcano heats up, melting snow and ice can mix with loose rock, creating huge mudflows called lahars. About fifty-six hundred years ago, one of these flows reached the Puget Sound lowlands, where towns like Auburn stand today. [confident] Many communities now sit on old lahar deposits, so the valleys have warning sirens and marked evacuation routes. [curious] And Rainier last erupted only about a thousand years ago.
