=== FACEBOOK ===
Just over 200 years ago, a ditch dug mostly by hand turned New York City into America's busiest port.
The Erie Canal opened in 1825, running about 363 miles across upstate New York from Albany on the Hudson River to Buffalo on Lake Erie. Critics mocked it as "Clinton's Ditch," after Governor DeWitt Clinton. Because the land rises about 570 feet heading west, builders needed 83 locks to lift boats up and over. Once it opened, the cost of shipping goods fell by roughly 90 percent. To celebrate, Clinton sailed down to New York and poured a keg of Lake Erie water into the Atlantic - the "Wedding of the Waters."
