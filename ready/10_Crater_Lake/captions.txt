The deepest lake in America sits inside a volcano that collapsed.
About 7,700 years ago, a towering volcano called Mount Mazama erupted in Oregon's Cascade Mountains. It blasted out so much ash and magma that the top of the mountain fell in on itself, leaving a bowl roughly five miles across called a caldera. Over centuries, rain and snow filled it. No rivers flow in and none flow out. Today Crater Lake is 1,943 feet deep, the deepest lake in the United States, and because nearly all its water falls straight from the sky, it's one of the clearest, bluest lakes on Earth.
